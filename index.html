    Privacy and limits

    • On this device only keeps the stub in Chrome. The other read modes may send it to Google to be read. The basic reader never leaves the page.
    • Nothing is saved or logged by this page.
    • Rates are for 2026, Canada outside Quebec, one employer: CPP 5.95% above a 3,500 yearly exemption up to 74,600; CPP2 4% from 74,600 to 85,000; EI 1.63% up to 68,900 (CRA, checked Nov 14, 2026).
    • Income tax is not checked, because it depends on your TD1 claims. Taxable benefits and pay periods that span two years are not handled.
    • This is a check, not payroll advice. If something differs, ask your payroll team for the calculation.
